# USOP Core v1.0 - Feedback Questionnaire

**Document:** 06-Feedback-Questionnaire
**Release:** 0.14.0-dp2-final
**Status:** Frozen Design Partner Documentation
**Audience:** Design Partners, Security Analysts, Security Leaders, IAM Teams, Platform Teams

## Purpose

Thank you for participating in the USOP Core Design Partner Program.

This questionnaire is designed to collect structured, comparable feedback based on actual use of USOP Core v1.0.

Please answer from real operational experience whenever possible.

The objective is not to collect general opinions.

The objective is to understand whether USOP reduces analyst effort, improves situational awareness, improves decision quality, preserves useful organizational knowledge, and can be deployed and trusted in a real security environment.

## Feedback Success Criteria

> **Feedback should provide enough structured evidence to determine whether USOP Core reduces operational effort, improves situational awareness, improves decision quality, and earns enough trust that a security team would choose to keep using it.**

The objective is measurable product learning, not a collection of disconnected opinions.
